Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Ask us, we reply within a working day.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, logo, a few photos, and about 15 minutes to fill in our brief. If you don't have good photos, we will tell you what to shoot on your phone.

How long does it take?

About three weeks from your brief to launch. The biggest factor is how quickly you send feedback during review.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It is built on WordPress, the world's most popular website system, and it is yours. You can edit it yourself or have anyone else work on it.

What about the domain and hosting?

We help you buy them in your own name and pass the cost on at cost. If you already have them, we use yours.

Can I sell products online?

Our packages include order buttons for WhatsApp and the contact form, which works well for most small shops. Full online stores with cart and payments are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% at launch. We agree the price in writing before we start, so there are no surprises.
